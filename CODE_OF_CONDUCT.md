# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Circumstances a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Demonstrating empathy and respect
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing grace toward differing viewpoints

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, trolling, or derogatory comments
- Publishing others' private information
- Sexualized language or imagery in project spaces
- Other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Report incidents to `hello@aenimalabs.com`. Maintainers will review and respond
in a manner they deem appropriate to the circumstances, including warnings or
temporary/permanent bans from project spaces.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
